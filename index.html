Open the dashboard in a new tab

Business use case

Built for HR leaders, talent acquisition and retention teams, and executives who need to understand why employees leave. It analyses 1,470 employees, 237 of whom have left (16.1% attrition against an industry average of 10 to 15%). Attrition is broken down by department, job role and salary band, with a deep-dive page and gender and department filters, so HR can see where turnover is concentrated.

Primary business objectives

  • Attrition monitoring and benchmarking: track the attrition rate (16.1%) against the industry benchmark, alongside employees who left (237), headcount (1,470), average income (6,503 monthly) and average tenure of leavers (5.1 years).
  • Department and role risk: Sales (20.6%) and Human Resources (19.0%) lead by department. Laboratory Technician (23.9%) and Human Resources (23.1%) lead by job role, while Research Director is only 2.5%.
  • Compensation and retention: the Under 3k salary band loses 28.6% of its staff, falling to 8.9% in the Above 10k band, which supports pay reviews and retention incentives.
  • Deep-dive risk factors: compare attrition by age group (35.8% for ages 18 to 25), overtime (30.5% vs 10.4%), marital status, business travel and education field, then drill down with the decomposition tree.

Business use case

Built for customer support heads, payment operations leads and executives. It tracks service quality across 52,000 support tickets and shows where SLA, first contact resolution (FCR) and repeat contact targets are missed. Breaking performance down by month, product, issue category and root-cause dependency (Technology/Vendor, Bank/Network, Merchant, Internal Ops, Customer) shows which issues and partners drive poor customer satisfaction, so teams can set owners and 30-day targets.

Primary business objectives

  • Service level and resolution tracking: monitor SLA achievement (70.56%), first contact resolution (54.65%), 7-day repeat contact rate (23.02%) and average CSAT (4.15). The monthly view explains the May and June dip, when volume rose from about 7 to 8K tickets a month to over 10K.
  • Root-cause and issue prioritisation: identify the categories missing leadership targets (Refund Delayed, Transaction Pending, Merchant Settlement, Unauthorized Transaction) and trace them to their dependencies. Technology/Vendor and Bank/Network drive the weakest results.
  • Product and channel performance: compare volume and resolution hours across UPI (48.95% of tickets), Wallet, Credit Card, BNPL and Merchant QR, filtered by month, city tier and region.
  • Action planning and accountability: turn findings into 30-day actions with named owners, baselines and targets, such as lifting Refund Delayed SLA from 23% to 45% through vendor escalation.

Business use case

Built for sales directors, regional managers and executives who need revenue, order volume and customer value in one view. It covers ₹63.14M in revenue (about $657.66K) across 6,268 orders, with date, month, quarter and year filters. The data runs through a bronze, silver and gold pipeline in Microsoft Fabric into a star schema, and the report shows which categories, regions and customers drive the business.

Primary business objectives

  • Revenue and order trends: track monthly revenue (roughly ₹4.5M to ₹5.7M) against order counts, with average revenue per order (₹10.10K) and average quantity per order (8.97).
  • Product category mix: Electronics brings in ₹45M, about 71% of revenue. Office Supplies leads in orders and units (77% of quantity) but earns only ₹5M, which points to pricing and portfolio opportunities.
  • Regional performance: benchmark North (₹16.2M), West (₹16.1M), East (₹15.9M) and South (₹14.9M) on revenue and order count to find under-performing markets.
  • Key customers: rank the top 10 customers by revenue and orders, led by Vivek Chopra at ₹1.97M, to support account management and retention.

Business use case

A Streamlit app for exploring the Property market in Gurgaon city, built on scraped and cleaned listings of flats and independent houses. It helps home buyers, sellers, investors and analysts estimate a fair price, understand how prices vary across sectors, and find comparable apartments.

Primary objectives

  • Price prediction: a Random Forest model (R² of about 0.90) estimates a price range in crores from property type, sector, bedrooms, bathrooms, balconies, age, built-up area, furnishing, luxury category and floor category. The range widens for higher-priced properties, where uncertainty is greater.
  • Market analysis: interactive charts show average price per sq ft by sector on a map, amenity word clouds, area against price, BHK distribution and a luxury score map, with filters by sector and property type.
  • Recommendations: search listings within a chosen radius of a location, or pick an apartment to get the top 5 most similar listings ranked by a similarity score on location, amenities and pricing.
  • Data pipeline: listings were scraped, deduplicated and cleaned (missing values and outliers), then engineered into model-ready features.
